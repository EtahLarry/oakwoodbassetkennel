Pricing & policies

Clear expectations make better beginnings.

Sample pricing for this demo site is $2,800. The final amount, deposit terms, and dates are always confirmed in writing before a commitment is made.

Included

Veterinary exam, age-appropriate vaccines and deworming, microchip, registration paperwork, starter food, blanket, and lifetime breeder support.

Deposit

A fully refundable deposit is required to reserve a puppy after the match has been confirmed. The deposit secures the puppy for the buyer while the reservation is active. If the buyer changes their mind at any point, the deposit will be fully refunded, with no cancellation penalty. The remaining balance is due before or at pickup, as outlined in the purchase agreement.
