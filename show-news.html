Featured champion

Grand Champion Basset Hound Tara!

Our girl “Tara” had multiple Best of Breed wins recently to finish her AKC Conformation Championship. She joins her sister Agatha in our Champion rolls here at the farm.

We went on to enter Tara in several larger exhibitions where she competed against other Basset Champions. She has now earned AKC Grand Champion status, and we could not be prouder of this remarkable girl.

New champion

Champion Basset Hound Klarissa!

Another of our show girls completed her Championship in Little Rock. Stonewall Klarissa finished at 10 months of age with four Majors and several Best of Breed wins, including two wins over top-ranked competition.

Her temperament is bubbly and she is a beautiful girl. Klarissa will be a wonderful addition to our program at Stonewall Farm, and we are excited to see what comes next for her.

Bassethound Total Eclipse of the Heart

Beautiful, elegant, and simply gorgeous, this is how “Eclipse” has been described.

She finished her AKC Championship with four major wins well before her first birthday. We anxiously await her offspring in the next year and look forward to seeing her continue to grow as a valued part of our program.
