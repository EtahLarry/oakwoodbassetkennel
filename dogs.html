Our standard

Beautiful is only the beginning.

We believe responsible breeding asks a bigger question than “What does this dog look like?” We consider movement, health, behavior, family history, and the lived experience of the puppy in a home.

  • Health testing appropriate to the breed and individual dogs
  • Thoughtful pairings with temperament and structure in mind
  • Open records and honest conversations with future families
